About us

A new company with a lion's resolve.

Siyabonga Bhubesi Group (Pty) Ltd is a recently established company based in Kwa-Thema, Johannesburg. We started with two things our community needs: dependable security services, and practical skills for young people.

Our scooter training business teaches young South Africans to ride safely and with confidence. Riders who complete the training can then rent scooters from us, which puts mobility and work within reach.

Bhubesi is the Zulu word for lion. It stands for the courage, pride and protection we bring to every service.

Mr Siyabonga BhubesiFounder

Scooter training programme

Learn to ride. Then ride to earn.

Many young people have the drive to work but no way to get around. Our programme trains them to ride scooters properly and safely, and then puts a scooter in their hands.

Training currently takes place in Kwa-Thema, Johannesburg. Get in touch to find out about the next intake.
